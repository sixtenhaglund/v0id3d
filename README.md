# V0ID3D

**Play it here: https://sixtenhaglund.github.io/v0id3d/**

You are a poor soul made of red glitch — and you are not the hero. The universe is
protecting itself with **Purification** (blue light that creeps in from the edges), and
your job is to **corrupt reality**: rip the purified shards off their podiums, corrupt
them at **The Anchor**, and bring them back to corrupt every podium before the
Purification swallows you.

## Layers

- **OUTSKIRTS**: the first reality. Beamers, Bashers, Thundermaws, mines, seas of
  Purification, Dangerzones, a Lightning Round — and **THE FENIX**.
- **SOLID SPACE**: planets with gravity, asteroid fields, whole star systems, dark space
  with black holes — and **THE SPACE ARACHNID**.
- **ANCIENT RUINS**: stone ruins, temples and a pitch-dark cave. Every enemy wears stone
  armour, and the **GOLEMS** punch and throw boulders. At the end waits **THE ANCIENT SOUL**.

## Controls

| Key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| WASD / arrow keys | move |
| Shift | dash |
| E | rip a shard off its podium, corrupt it at The Anchor, corrupt a podium, use a Locator, take a red shard from a red rift |
| Q | drop the shard you're holding |
| Space (hold) | shield, while you hold a corrupted shard: lasers bounce back |
| 1 2 3 4 / mouse wheel | pick an inventory slot |
| Click / C | stab with a red shard — or fire your corrupted shard's beam (3 shots) |
| Right click / X (hold) | charge up and throw a red shard (it breaks on what it hits) |
| F | fullscreen |
| M | mute |
| Esc / P | pause |

Click the screen first — browsers only allow sound after you click.
It works on phones too, with on-screen controls.

## Red rifts

Every sector hides 3 to 5 small **red rifts**. Press E on one to pull out a sharp red
shard that works like a sword: stab with it (4 hits), or hold to charge a throw that
flies further, faster and harder — but shatters on whatever it hits.

## Level editor

Press **L** on the title screen to build your own levels: podiums, every enemy, ruins,
golems, traps, walls, planets, triggers, music — and your own enemies with the Enemy
Maker. Share a level with its code.
